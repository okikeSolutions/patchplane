# Keep task acceptance requirements under maintainer authority

Accepted during the V2 design interview on 2026-11-07. Acceptance Requirements combine repository defaults with task-specific requirements approved by the maintainer. Agents can propose changes but cannot silently remove requirements or declare their own evidence sufficient. Approval is explicit on a structured proposal attached to the PR/MR and binds to that proposal's exact version. Editing a proposal does not silently preserve approval. This separates implementation from acceptance authority at the cost of an explicit approval step. The approval mechanism and handling of implementation-only pushes, repository-default changes and additional verification checks remain open design decisions.

Updated by Q20 on 2026-11-22, recorded in ADR 0006: an authorized maintainer may approve through any authenticated presentation interface. The proposal remains attached to the PR/MR; approval is not restricted to the forge interface. Handling implementation-only pushes, repository-default changes and additional verification checks remains open.

Q11 resolves push handling: requirement approval survives implementation-only pushes, but execution for the previous candidate is cancelled and the new candidate must be verified. Changes to requirements, activated extension versions or execution authority require renewed approval. Previous results remain historical evidence. Q12 introduces configurable approval rules; whether standing authorization may satisfy these renewed approvals is still open, as recorded in ADR 0007.
