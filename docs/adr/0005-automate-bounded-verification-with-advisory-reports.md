# Automate bounded verification and keep dogfood reports advisory

Accepted during the V2 design interview on 2026-11-07. Approved requirements and relevant subsequent pushes trigger bounded verification automatically, with cancellation of superseded work. Expensive or privileged runs require separate authorization. Initial dogfooding publishes advisory reports rather than enforcing merge restrictions, while missing required evidence remains incomplete. This permits real verification and operational learning before trusting the system as a merge gate. Resource budgets, privileged-run definitions, trigger details and cancellation recovery remain open decisions.

Q13, answered on 2026-11-22, selects the first dogfood limits: one active attempt per PR/MR, thirty minutes of active execution per attempt excluding approval and device-queue waits, and a US$5 model-spend ceiling per attempt. At most one infrastructure-failure retry is allowed within the same total budget. Failed acceptance checks do not trigger automatic retries. Native access uses T3/SimLock's queue; heavy builds remain separately coordinated. These limits are trial choices, not measured execution requirements. Accounting, enforcement and cleanup behavior at the limit still need definition.

Q12 makes approval prompting configurable under user-owned policy, recorded in ADR 0007. This does not remove authorization or platform enforcement. Trigger classification, cancellation recovery and policy treatment of privileged operations remain open.

Q25 resolves budget exhaustion: stop verification and leave remaining requirements incomplete. Cleanup continues under a separate bounded allowance, and an environment with unconfirmed cleanup cannot be reused. Automatic execution under the US$5 model-spend ceiling requires enforceable accounting. If the provider cannot support that ceiling, an alternative limit needs explicit authorization; unknown spend must not be counted as zero. The numerical cleanup allowance remains open.

Q29 permits an explicit Stop action to cancel the current attempt independently of the rule that configuration edits affect future attempts. Cleanup gets an initial five-minute allowance. Failed or timed-out cleanup quarantines the environment and creates a Decision Inbox item. A quarantine is not a successful deletion or cleanup claim.

Updated on 2026-11-23: drop SimLock on personal devices as a security-by-design rule; Arbeo Android runs strictly in the cloud behind a cloud device adapter (concrete provider open until stack proof). Where verifier model calls do not pass through a dollar-metering gateway, enforce explicit turn, token, and wall-clock ceilings rather than blocking or treating unknown spend as zero. See [ADR 0010](0010-postgres-single-store-and-cloud-only-verification.md).
